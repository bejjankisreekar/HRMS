FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the free trial?

Every new organization gets a 14-day free trial with full access to core modules. No credit card required.

Can I migrate data from another HR system?

Yes. We support CSV imports for employees, attendance, and leave balances. {{ plan_names.growth }} plan includes migration assistance.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each organization is isolated in a multi-tenant architecture with role-based access controls.

Do you support multiple locations or branches?

Yes. HRMS Suite supports multi-branch organizations with department-level reporting and location-based attendance rules.

What integrations are available?

REST API and JWT auth are built in. Payroll exports, biometric devices, and SSO integrations are available on {{ plan_names.professional }} and {{ plan_names.growth }} plans.
